01Which cars do you buy in Jeddah?

We buy all makes and models: running, non-running, accident-damaged, flooded, fire-damaged, old and junk cars. That includes sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs and pickups, whatever their age or mileage.

02How is the price of my scrap car decided?

Your offer is based on the make, model, year, overall condition, demand for its usable parts and its metal value. Sending clear photos along with your details helps us give the most accurate price.

03Do you buy cars that do not start?

Yes. Non-running cars are welcome, and you do not need to repair anything first. Just tell us what the problem is, such as a seized engine or a failed gearbox, and we will buy the car as it is and tow it for free.

04Do you buy accident-damaged, flooded or fire-damaged cars?

Yes. We buy cars with front, rear or side damage, total-loss vehicles and cars damaged by water or fire. You get a fair price based on what the car and its usable parts are worth, with no repairs needed.

05What documents do I need to sell my scrap car?

Usually proof of ownership and your ID. Requirements can vary by car, so message us before pickup and we will confirm exactly what is needed.

06Is the offer final?

The offer is based on the details and photos you share. If the car matches the description when we collect it, the agreed price stays the same.

07What happens to my car after you buy it?

Usable parts from the cars we buy are inspected and supplied as quality used spare parts in Jeddah. This gives end-of-life vehicles a second life and keeps used parts affordable for drivers and workshops.
