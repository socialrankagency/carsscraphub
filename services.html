Which services does CarsScrapHub offer?

We offer four core services: scrap car buying, free scrap car removal, used auto spare parts and used car buying, all across Jeddah.

Which cars do you buy in Jeddah?

We buy all makes and models: running, non-running, accident-damaged, flooded, fire-damaged, old and junk cars. That includes sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs and pickups, whatever their age or mileage.

How is the price of my car decided?

Your offer is based on the make, model, year, overall condition, demand for its usable parts and its metal value. Sending clear photos along with your details helps us give the most accurate price.

Is scrap car removal really free?

Yes. Pickup and towing from anywhere in Jeddah are free once you accept our offer. There is no towing fee and nothing to pay for the removal.

Do I need to arrange a tow truck for a car that will not start?

No. We tow non-running, damaged and stuck cars ourselves at no cost, so you do not need to arrange a tow truck or fix anything before selling.

How do I get paid for my car?

Payment is made when we collect the car, once you have agreed on the price. There are no hidden deductions and no waiting.

What documents do I need to sell my car?

Usually proof of ownership and your ID. Requirements can vary by car, so message us before pickup and we will confirm exactly what is needed.

How do I request a used car part?

Open the Sell My Car page, choose Buy a used car part, and add your car details and the part you need. We check our yard and reply on WhatsApp with availability and price.
